My story

I grew up online, earlier than most people around me. Where I’m from, not many people were tuned in to internet culture, so I spent a lot of time on the other side of that gap. I was obsessed with films, music and art, and with the idea of making something that was entirely mine.

When I was fourteen, the pandemic pushed everyone onto screens. I was already there. That early access taught me taste: what stops a thumb on a poster, what makes a brand feel expensive, what makes a night feel like an event before it starts. I know that access was a privilege, and I’ve tried to use it properly.

I’m at my best when an idea in my head becomes something people can see in the real world. When I get into the zone, I learn fast. For the last two years I’ve used that to design for clubs, cafes, fashion and jewellery brands, and artists. Wherever the work involves creative thinking, curation or art, that’s where I want to be.
